This is **0.1.2** (not released yet), a fix release after
[0.1.1](https://github.com/bnelair/mef3io-studio/releases/tag/v0.1.1).

## Fixed

- **The signal viewer is fast again.** Paging, scrolling, changing the
  size of the signals or the montage took 2 to 4 seconds with many
  channels, and the window froze meanwhile. Each step now takes about a
  tenth of a second, and the first page appears in about half a second
  (measured with 64 channels). Traces may look slightly finer on standard
  screens; on high-resolution screens they look as before.
- On screens scaled to 125% or 175%, moving the pointer over the signals no
  longer redraws them each time.
