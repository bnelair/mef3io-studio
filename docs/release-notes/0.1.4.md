This is **0.1.4** (not released yet), after
[0.1.3](https://github.com/bnelair/mef3io-studio/releases/tag/v0.1.3).

## New

- **Join or trim can make the new recording anonymous, and save it as one
  file, in a single go.** A new step, **Privacy**, comes after **What to
  keep**: keep the patient details (as before), or **Make it anonymous**,
  with the Anonymize page's own choices for one recording: keep the real
  dates or move every date by the same number of whole days, a pseudonym,
  what to clear, and whether to remove or keep the text of notes and events.
  The **New recording** step has a new **Save as** choice: a folder
  (`.mefd`), or **one file (`.mefd.tar`), which this app opens directly**.
  An anonymous recording is made straight from the originals, trimmed and
  without the channels you left out: a copy with the patient's details is
  never written to your computer, and no second trip through the Anonymize
  page is needed.
- **Free space is checked before anything is written** for the busiest moment
  of the chosen steps, and the message says how much room is needed.
- **One progress bar for all the steps**, each named in plain words (keeping
  the chosen part of each recording, joining, packing, checking the file).
  **Stop** works at every step and leaves nothing behind.

## Changed

- Join or trim has one more step (Privacy), so "New recording" is now step 5.
