This is **0.1.4**, after
[0.1.3](https://github.com/bnelair/mef3io-studio/releases/tag/v0.1.3): Join or
trim makes one new recording straight from the originals in one go (channels
left out, trimmed, anonymous if you like, with or without notes, as one file if
you like), the part to keep can be chosen on the map, and a user manual with
tutorial videos comes with the release.

Installers for Windows, macOS (14 or later) and Linux are attached below,
with the user manual (PDF) and the tutorial videos. Check your download
against the `SHA256SUMS` file.

> **These builds are not code-signed yet.** Your computer will ask you to
> confirm the first start:
>
> - **Windows:** "Windows protected your PC" → *More info* → *Run anyway*.
> - **Mac:** open the app once and close the warning, then go to *System
>   Settings → Privacy & Security* and click *Open Anyway* next to the message
>   about mef3io-studio. (On macOS 14: right-click the app → *Open*.) If
>   macOS says the app "is damaged and can't be opened", the download was
>   quarantined: move the app to *Applications*, run
>   `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/mef3io-studio.app` in
>   Terminal, then open it again.
> - **Linux:** make the AppImage executable (*Properties → Allow executing*),
>   then double-click it.

## New

- **A user manual (PDF) and captioned tutorial videos** come with every
  release. They show joining and trimming, making a recording anonymous and
  saving it as one file, step by step, on made-up recordings; the app itself
  makes the pictures and the videos, so they match the screens you see.

- **Choose what to keep on a map.** In Join or trim, the **What to keep** step
  now shows the same map as Explore's combined timeline (when each recording
  has data, with zoom and moving along it) with two lines, **Start** and
  **End**, that you drag with the mouse or move with the keyboard (S or E to
  pick a line, then the arrow keys). Below it, Start and End each have a
  **date and time (UTC, to the microsecond)** and a **time as a number
  (microseconds since 1 January 1970, UTC)**; the lines and both fields
  always show the same moment, and a time that cannot be read says so and
  moves nothing. The window applies to every chosen recording: each keeps only
  its data inside it, and a recording with no data there is greyed on the map
  and **left out**, with a note. This is the default when you choose several
  recordings; **Choose for each recording** is still there (the map then shows
  what each keeps as shaded parts). The times are the real times of the
  recordings, and the step says so when you make the new recording anonymous
  with moved dates.

- **Join or trim can make the new recording anonymous, and save it as one
  file, in a single go.** A new step, **Privacy**, comes after **What to
  keep**: keep the patient details (as before), or **Make it anonymous**,
  with the Anonymize page's own choices for one recording: keep the real
  dates or move every date by the same number of whole days, a pseudonym,
  what to clear, and whether to remove or keep the text of notes and events.
  The **New recording** step has a new **Save as** choice: a folder
  (`.mefd`), or **one file (`.mefd.tar`), which this app opens directly**.
  An anonymous recording is made straight from the originals, trimmed and
  without the channels you left out: a copy with the patient's details is
  never written to your computer, and no second trip through the Anonymize
  page is needed.
- **Free space is checked before anything is written** for the busiest moment
  of the chosen steps, and the message says how much room is needed.
- **One progress bar for all the steps**, each named in plain words (keeping
  the chosen part of each recording, joining, packing, checking the file).
  **Stop** works at every step and leaves nothing behind.

- **Join or trim: notes and events, keep or leave out.** The **Privacy**
  step has a new choice, **Notes and events: Keep them / Leave them out**
  (Keep is the default). Leaving them out removes every note and event from
  the new recording, with its time, at every level; the signal, times and
  channels are as without it. It works for any new recording, anonymous or
  not (for an anonymous one that keeps them, the choice to remove or keep
  their text still applies). The result screen says when they were left out.
- **Join or trim makes the new recording in one pass.** On mef3io 2.2.0 the
  kept part of each recording, the channels left out, making it anonymous, the
  notes choice and the new passwords all happen in one pass over the data,
  straight from the originals: no temporary copy of any part is written
  any more (so the hidden folder is only used to pack **one file**), the
  originals are read once to make the new recording and once to check it, and
  far fewer files are opened, which helps most on a network drive. Free
  space needed is now the size of the new recording (twice that for one
  file). **Stop** still leaves nothing behind. A recording with notes that was
  cut and joined to another no longer fails its own check.

## Changed

- The app now needs **mef3io 2.2.0** (it was 2.1.1).

- Join or trim has one more step (Privacy), so "New recording" is now step 5.
- The small preview of kept parts under the list on **What to keep** is replaced
  by the map.
