This is **0.1.5**: not released yet.

## Fixed

- **"Checking everything before starting" in Join or trim does less work
  on big recordings.** Studio no longer opens every recording again or walks
  through all of its folders (that took tens of thousands of file requests).
  It uses what Explore already knows about each recording (when it starts and
  ends, its channels and its size), and touches each recording at most once
  per action: it looks at a few files only to see whether the recording has
  changed since Explore read it, which includes a recording that has grown
  while the page was open. If it has changed, the page says so, checks again
  and uses the new times. The library's own check and copy still read the
  recording (once in the check, once in the run); how much work they do is
  improved in the library's own release, not here. No time saving is promised.
- **Sending a range from the signal viewer to Join or trim now keeps your
  channel choice.** Join or trim stops at the **Channels** step, with the
  channels you could see in the viewer kept and the ones you hid left out, so
  you can see and change which channels go into the new recording. If you
  hid every channel, none is ticked yet and the step says so.

## Changed

- **Choosing a few channels is easier to find.** The channel step now says
  that a ticked box means "kept" and explains how to keep only a few: choose
  **Exclude all**, then tick the channels you want.
