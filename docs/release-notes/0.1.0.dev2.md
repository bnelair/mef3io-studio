This is a **test version** for students and researchers who work with MEF 3.0
recordings. It is not the finished app: some features are still missing, and
we would like to hear what is unclear or does not work.

Installers for Windows, macOS (14 or later) and Linux are attached below.
Check your download against the `SHA256SUMS` file.

> **These builds are not code-signed yet.** Your computer will ask you to
> confirm the first start:
>
> - **Windows:** "Windows protected your PC" → *More info* → *Run anyway*.
> - **Mac:** open the app once and close the warning, then go to *System
>   Settings → Privacy & Security* and click *Open Anyway* next to the message
>   about mef3io-studio. (On macOS 14: right-click the app → *Open*.) If
>   macOS says the app "is damaged and can't be opened", the download was
>   quarantined: move the app to *Applications*, run
>   `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/mef3io-studio.app` in
>   Terminal, then open it again.
> - **Linux:** make the AppImage executable (*Properties → Allow executing*),
>   then double-click it.

## What's new since 0.1.0.dev1

**A new Anonymize page.** It makes anonymized copies of your recordings: the
patient details are removed and every date and time is moved by the same
amount. **Your original recordings are never changed.** The app makes a new
copy, checks it, and only then keeps it. A step-by-step guide takes you
through it, and nothing is saved until the last step.

- **Choose the channels** to keep. Even with 256 channels this is quick:
  channels are grouped by electrode, and there is a filter, Shift+click for
  a range, and Undo.
- **Move the dates.** You choose a new date for one recording, and the
  others follow it, so they keep their order and spacing. Or every copy can
  start at the same moment.
- **Name the copies** and give each one a pseudonym (a code for the
  patient). The app checks that no name contains the patient's details.
- **Remove or keep the text of notes.** It is removed by default. If you
  keep it, read it yourself before sharing: dates and names written in free
  text are not found automatically.
- **Add two new passwords to the copies**, or none (the usual choice for
  sharing).
- **Save the copies** as folders, as one file per recording, or as one
  archive for the whole batch to send to someone.
- **See the original and new dates** of every recording at the end. Anyone
  with this list can undo the anonymization, so the app saves it to a file
  only if you ask.

**Long batches can run on their own.** Start a batch of many recordings and
walk away. One problem does not stop the batch, the computer is kept awake
(you can turn this off in **File → Settings**; on a Mac, Settings is in the
mef3io-studio menu), and a notification tells you when it is done. If the app
crashes or the computer loses power, the next start offers to continue the
batch. A batch into one archive can also be continued after you stop it or
close the app, once at least one recording is in it. In **Settings** you can also choose the **work folder** where
each copy is made and checked before it is packed.

**A Privacy check in Explore.** For any recording, original or copy, it shows
every kind of detail that could identify the patient, each marked as empty
or found, and whether the anonymizer made the recording.

**Several recordings on one timeline.** Choose **Combined timeline** in
Explore to see recordings side by side, one row each. Time that two
recordings share is marked, and the time with data is added up.

**Save times as a table.** Save the start and end of your recordings as a
CSV file that opens in Excel or LibreOffice. Every time is written in a form
people can read and in forms that scripts read exactly.

**Real progress and a working Stop.** Opening and packing large recordings
now show how much is really done, and **Stop task** stops the work straight
away, even in the middle of a large recording.

**Good to know:** as before, times are shown in **UTC** until you choose the
time zone of the place where the recordings were made (**View → Time
zone…**).

## What to try

Please use copies of recordings you are allowed to work with, or test
recordings without patient data.

1. **Open or scan a folder of recordings** on the Explore page. Look at the
   details of one recording, then select several and choose **Combined
   timeline**.
2. **Save their times as a table** and open it in your spreadsheet program.
   Does it look right?
3. **Anonymize two or three recordings** into a new, empty folder. Leave out a
   few channels, move the dates, and look at the result page.
4. Choose **Open the copy** and read its **Privacy check**. Does it say
   clearly that nothing was found?
5. **Try a batch into one archive** and stop it halfway with **Stop**. Then
   either choose **Finish the archive with what is done**, or close the app,
   start it again and continue the batch when it asks.
6. Tell us **which words or steps were confusing**. That is as useful to us
   as a bug.

The [user guide](https://github.com/bnelair/mef3io-studio/blob/main/docs/user-guide.md)
explains every step.

## Known limitations

- **Not code-signed** (see above).
- **Dark theme:** buttons you choose one option from (radio buttons) are
  barely visible when they are not selected
  ([#35](https://github.com/bnelair/mef3io-studio/issues/35)). The light
  theme (**View → Appearance → Light**) shows them clearly.
- **Not in this version yet:** sorting and renaming recordings, viewing and
  editing their details, adding or removing passwords on a recording, and
  saving a choice of channels to reuse. A signal viewer comes later.
- **One choice for all note text:** the text of notes is either removed from
  every copy or kept in every copy. Choosing per note is not possible yet.
- **Copies are made one after another,** not several at once.
- **One archive for the whole batch** cannot be opened in the app directly.
  Extract it first with any archive tool (7-Zip, the one built into Windows
  or macOS, or `tar`); you then get the recordings back as usual.
- **There is no saved report** of an anonymization yet, apart from the list
  of original and new dates (saved only if you ask).
- **Continuing an interrupted batch:** if a copy already exists at the
  destination and you choose **Make them again**, the app checks that it made
  that copy itself (its name, dates, channels and passwords), but not yet
  whether the patient details and note text were treated the same way
  ([#36](https://github.com/bnelair/mef3io-studio/issues/36)). This matters
  only if another copy of the same recording, with the same name and dates,
  appeared there while the batch was stopped.

## Reporting a problem

Open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/bnelair/mef3io-studio/issues/new/choose).
Issues are **public**, so:

- **Never attach a recording**, a screenshot that shows patient details, or
  real names, dates or folder names. Describe what you did and what you saw
  instead.
- Say which **version** you use (**Help → About mef3io-studio**; on a Mac, in the mef3io-studio menu) and on which
  computer (Windows, Mac or Linux).
- If the app itself does not seem to work, **Help → Check this
  installation** tests it with a small made-up recording; tell us what it
  says.
- The app's log (**Help → Open the log folder**) leaves out patient details,
  names, folder paths and dates. Still, read it before you attach it.

If you find that an anonymized copy still contains patient details or a real
date, please **do not open a public issue**. Report it privately instead:
[report a privacy problem](https://github.com/bnelair/mef3io-studio/security/advisories/new).
