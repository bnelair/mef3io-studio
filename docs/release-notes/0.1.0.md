This is **0.1.0, the first full release** of mef3io-studio. It is no
longer a test version: it is the latest release on the
[Releases page](https://github.com/bnelair/mef3io-studio/releases), and it
is still an early version, so we would like to hear what is unclear or does
not work. It builds on
[0.1.0.dev6](https://github.com/bnelair/mef3io-studio/releases/tag/v0.1.0.dev6).

Installers for Windows, macOS (14 or later) and Linux are attached below.
Check your download against the `SHA256SUMS` file.

> **These builds are not code-signed yet.** Your computer will ask you to
> confirm the first start:
>
> - **Windows:** "Windows protected your PC" → *More info* → *Run anyway*.
> - **Mac:** open the app once and close the warning, then go to *System
>   Settings → Privacy & Security* and click *Open Anyway* next to the message
>   about mef3io-studio. (On macOS 14: right-click the app → *Open*.) If
>   macOS says the app "is damaged and can't be opened", the download was
>   quarantined: move the app to *Applications*, run
>   `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/mef3io-studio.app` in
>   Terminal, then open it again.
> - **Linux:** make the AppImage executable (*Properties → Allow executing*),
>   then double-click it.

## What the app does

- **Explore** recordings without loading any signal: channels, times, gaps,
  notes, history, a check for problems, several recordings on one time line,
  a table of times (CSV), and a Privacy check.
- **Anonymize** recordings into new, checked copies, one or many, also as
  unattended batches that can be continued after a crash.
- **Join or trim**: keep part of a recording, or join parts of several into
  one new recording.
- **View signals** of one or several recordings as one, with montages,
  filters, notes and events, and an optional edit mode to add notes.
- **Rename** recordings safely, **edit details** into a new copy, give new
  copies **passwords**, and **make new recordings** from many at once.
- **Pack** a recording into one file, or **unpack** it.

The originals are never changed, except when you rename them or, in edit
mode, add notes after you confirm. Nothing leaves your computer.

## What's new since 0.1.0.dev6

- **Rename recordings safely, in time order.** The **Rename** window lists
  the selected recordings by when they were recorded, builds names from a
  pattern (or lets you type them), checks every name before anything
  happens (too long, not allowed, already taken, two recordings with the
  same name), and renames the folder, the notes of the whole recording and
  the names kept inside the files together. Each recording is renamed on its
  own; if one cannot be, the others go on. **Undo the last rename** puts the
  old names back.
- **Edit details (makes a new copy).** Change the patient's name and ID, the
  place, the recording description and the channel descriptions. The result
  is a new recording that the app checks against the original; the original
  stays as it is. Dates and times cannot be changed, and a text that is too
  long is refused, never cut.
- **Password choices on every new copy.** Anonymize, Join or trim and Edit
  details all offer the same choice: no password, or two new ones. The
  originals' passwords are never carried over, and passwords are never
  stored.
- **Saved channel choices and one "Make new recordings" batch.** Save a
  choice of channels under a name, share it as a small file, and apply it to
  many recordings at once, together with details and new passwords. It runs
  as an unattended batch with progress, Stop, and a way to continue after a
  crash.
- **Built on mef3io 2.1.1.**

## Known limitations

- **Not code-signed** (see above).
- **No changes in place.** Details and channels are changed only by making a
  new copy. Changing or removing a note that is **already stored** is not
  possible yet; edit mode only adds notes.
- **Cutting one recording into several pieces** is not available yet.
  Keeping a part and joining parts of several recordings is (Join or trim).
- **Undo of a rename lasts until the app is closed.** After that, rename the
  recordings back by hand.
- **Passwords** are chosen only for new copies. Adding or removing the
  password of an existing recording is not possible.
- **Copies are made one after another,** not several at once.
- The limits of the earlier versions that still apply (joining and trimming
  rules, one choice for all note text when anonymizing, one archive for a
  whole batch, no saved anonymization report, very short names when
  continuing a batch) are listed in the
  [0.1.0.dev6 notes](https://github.com/bnelair/mef3io-studio/releases/tag/v0.1.0.dev6)
  and explained in the
  [user guide](https://github.com/bnelair/mef3io-studio/blob/main/docs/user-guide.md).

## What to try

Please use copies of recordings you are allowed to work with, or test
recordings without patient data. Rename a few of them and undo it, make a
copy with changed details, and make new recordings from several at once.
Tell us **which words or steps were confusing**: that is as useful to us as
a bug. Settings (**File → Settings**) and About (**Help → About
mef3io-studio**) are in the mef3io-studio menu on a Mac.

## Reporting a problem

Open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/bnelair/mef3io-studio/issues/new/choose).
Issues are **public**, so:

- **Never attach a recording**, a screenshot that shows patient details, or
  real names, dates or folder names. Describe what you did and what you saw
  instead.
- Say which **version** you use (**Help → About mef3io-studio**; on a Mac, in
  the mef3io-studio menu) and on which computer (Windows, Mac or Linux).
- If the app itself does not seem to work, **Help → Check this
  installation** tests it with a small made-up recording; tell us what it
  says.
- The app's log (**Help → Open the log folder**) leaves out patient details,
  names, folder paths and dates. Still, read it before you attach it.

If you find that an anonymized copy still contains patient details or a real
date, please **do not open a public issue**. Report it privately instead:
[report a privacy problem](https://github.com/bnelair/mef3io-studio/security/advisories/new).
