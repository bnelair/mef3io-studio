# Not released yet (0.1.6)

- **Studio now uses mef3io 2.4.0** (it was 2.3.0). What you notice: when you
  open a recording whose notes and events were being changed when a program
  was interrupted, **Explore** says the notes and events cannot be read right
  now (instead of showing none), **Check for problems** says what happened
  and what to do next (the recording must be rescued before its notes and
  events can be used again; this app version does not do that itself yet),
  and Join or trim, Edit details and Anonymize refuse it in words that fit
  (nothing is written). Leftover files from such a change are reported too.
  Nothing else changes yet.

- **Overview of the whole recording in the signal viewer.** A band above the
  coloured timeline bar shows the lowest and highest value of a channel over
  the whole recording, so you can see where to go before the slow loading of
  a page. The frame of the current page reaches up over the band, and clicking
  or dragging on it moves there. The **Overview** button chooses the channels
  (the first shown by default, up to four, each scaled to its own size);
  channels you hide leave the band. It is read in the background from the
  recording that is already open, never loading the signals.
