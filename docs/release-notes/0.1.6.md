# Not released yet (0.1.6)

- **One button to start, in every wizard.** Anonymize, Join or trim, Make new,
  Cut into pieces and Edit details no longer have a separate "check" button
  to press, wait for, and then press again. The one button (for example
  **Save the new recording**) first checks everything without writing
  anything ("Step 1 of 2", on the page that follows, with the time since it
  started and **Stop**), and then makes the result ("Step 2 of 2"). What is
  written is exactly what was checked. If something stops it, every reason
  is listed in plain words and you are back at the last step with every
  choice kept; **Stop** in the check does the same, and a Stop you pressed
  always wins, even when the check was just finishing.
- **After a run.** **Back to my choices** keeps everything you chose, also
  after Stop, and **Show the last result** brings the outcome back until the
  next run really starts writing. A refusal or a Stop in step 1 never takes
  the last result (or **Open it in Explore**) away. Anonymize asks first when
  the key to the moved dates was not saved.
- **Passwords are never skipped by accident.** They are cleared when a run
  starts and when you leave the page. After that the step does not go on
  until you type them again or click a choice, "No password" included, and
  says why. Make new and Cut into pieces cannot be closed with X or Close
  while they work, and nothing is wiped by trying; **Stop** comes first.
- **Check for problems says what it really checks.** The tab offers **Quick
  look**, **Full check** and **Read everything back**, shows about how much
  each reads, a bar and **Stop**. The first two do not read the signal
  values themselves, and every "No problems" of theirs says so; only **Read
  everything back** can find damage inside the signal (it reads about as
  much as copying the recording). Results name the kind of check they come
  from.

- **Overview of the whole recording in the signal viewer.** A band above the
  coloured timeline bar shows the lowest and highest value of a channel over
  the whole recording, so you can see where to go before the slow loading of
  a page. The frame of the current page reaches up over the band, and clicking
  or dragging on it moves there. The **Overview** button chooses the channels
  (the first shown by default, up to four, each scaled to its own size);
  channels you hide leave the band. It is read in the background from the
  recording that is already open, never loading the signals.
